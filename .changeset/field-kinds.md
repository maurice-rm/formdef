`@formdef/field-kinds` adds field kinds for form documents: short and long text, email, a number with a unit, a whole number, a checkbox, single and multiple choice, a dropdown and a date; block kinds for a notice and a heading, rendered by the new `@formdef/notice` and `@formdef/heading`; and display kinds for the stepper's rail, segments and compact variants and a section that reads as one question.
