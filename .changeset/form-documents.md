The first release: describe a form as data, a form document, for form builders and forms stored in a database. `readDocument` and `checkDocument` check a document, `defineFieldKind` and `createFieldKinds` define what its fields may be, `createValidator` validates its answers in the form and on a server, `describeAnswers` describes them, edit operations change a document, and `createDocumentForm` from `@formdef/document/react` renders it through your registry.
