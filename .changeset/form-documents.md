First release: describe a form as data, a form document, for form builders and forms stored in a database. `readDocument` and `checkDocument` check a document, `readDocument` within limits on its size, `defineFieldKind` defines what its fields may be, `defineValueType` adds kinds of answer, with the operators conditions compare them by, to the built-in `valueTypes`, and `createCatalog` collects them, `createValidator` validates its answers in the form and on a server, `describeAnswers` describes them, edit operations change a document, and `createDocumentForm` from `@formdef/document/react` renders it through your registry.
