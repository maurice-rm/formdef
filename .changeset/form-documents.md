First release: describe a form as data, a form document, for form builders and forms stored in a database. `readDocument` and `checkDocument` check a document, `readDocument` within limits on its size, `defineFieldKind` defines what its fields may be, `defineBlockKind` content between them that gives no answer, with formatted text settings, `defineDisplayKind` how the form of steps, a step, a section, a row or a repeat looks, `defineValueType` adds kinds of answer, with the operators conditions compare them by, with a fixed value or another answer, to the built-in `valueTypes`, and `createCatalog` collects them, texts may be translated into the document's `languages`, follow an answer and name earlier answers as `{key}`, a field may carry its own error texts and checks against other answers, a form may open with an intro, steps jump forward on an answer or end the form, items repeat as entries the person adds and removes, `endingOf` tells which of the document's endings applies, computed values work things out from the answers, options may carry a score, descriptions render limited Markdown, `createValidator` validates its answers in the form and on a server, `describeAnswers` describes them, edit operations change a document, and `createDocumentForm` from `@formdef/document/react` renders it through your registry.
