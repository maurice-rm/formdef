First release: describe a form as data, a form document, for form builders and forms stored in a database. `readDocument` and `checkDocument` check a document, `readDocument` within limits on its size, `defineFieldKind` defines what its fields may be, `defineBlockKind` content between them that gives no answer, with formatted text settings, `defineDisplayKind` how the form of steps, a step, a section or a row looks, `defineValueType` adds kinds of answer, with the operators conditions compare them by, to the built-in `valueTypes`, and `createCatalog` collects them, texts may be translated into the document's `languages`, follow an answer and name earlier answers as `{key}`, a field may carry its own error texts, descriptions render limited Markdown, `createValidator` validates its answers in the form and on a server, `describeAnswers` describes them, edit operations change a document, and `createDocumentForm` from `@formdef/document/react` renders it through your registry.
